# Before you publish

This site and its source files are **public**. Anything you commit can be read, copied, and reused by anyone, and it stays in the history even if you delete it later. Work through this checklist before you open a pull request.

## Checklist

- [ ] **No personal information.** No names, phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers, photos of people, or anything that could identify a member, participant, or staff member. Use roles instead.
- [ ] **No secrets.** No passwords, API keys, access codes, bank details, or private links.
- [ ] **No internal-only links or files.** Links to private drives, internal systems, or shared inboxes do not belong here.
- [ ] **Forms and templates are blank.** Show the blank form, never a filled-in one.
- [ ] **You have the right to share it.** Do not paste text, images, or forms owned by someone else unless their licence allows it, and credit the source.
- [ ] **Images have descriptions.** Every image has alt text, and nobody in a photo is identifiable without their written consent.
- [ ] **Version number, owner, review date, and revision history are updated.**
- [ ] **You checked the preview link.** The page looks right, and the links work.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> If you realise that something private has been published, tell a maintainer straight away. Removing a file is not enough, because the information stays in the repository history and may need to be removed from it.
