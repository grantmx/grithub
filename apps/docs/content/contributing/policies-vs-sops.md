# Policies, SOPs, and forms

GRIT Hub keeps four kinds of document. Each does a different job, and mixing them up makes all of them harder to use.

| Document | What it establishes | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Policy** | The rules, principles, and requirements | The Refund Policy says when a refund is due. |
| **SOP** | How a specific activity must be carried out, by whom, and with what evidence | SOP-FIN-001 explains how to receive, assess, approve, and pay a refund request. |
| **Work instruction** | Detailed instructions for one task or one system | How to record a refund in the accounting system. |
| **Form or checklist** | The evidence that the procedure was followed | The appeal submission checklist. |

## Rules of thumb

- **A policy says what and why. An SOP says how.** Someone who is new to GRIT Hub should be able to read an SOP and know how to do the task, what evidence to keep, and who can approve or resolve an exception.
- **Thresholds and limits come from the approved Delegation of Authority or the relevant policy.** An SOP refers to the approval level or the role, and does not invent amounts of its own.
- **Name roles, not people.** The SOP then stays correct when people change.
- **Not everything belongs in an SOP.** Governing documents, legal agreements, and statutory compliance documents stay as they are. SOPs explain how GRIT Hub puts them into practice.
- **Keep an SOP practical,** usually two to five pages, with forms or checklists where they help.
- **If a policy and an SOP disagree,** the policy applies until the difference is resolved.
