## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how GRIT Hub policies and SOPs are written, reviewed, approved, published, changed, and retired, so that everyone follows the same process and uses the current version. It applies to every document on this site and to everyone who proposes changes.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Author** | Drafts or updates the document and opens a pull request. |
| **Process owner** | Accountable for the document being correct and current. |
| **Approver** | Approves the change. For policies, this is the authority named in the policy. For SOPs, see [TBC: per the Delegation of Authority]. |
| **Site maintainer** | Looks after the repository, navigation, and publishing. |

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

1. **Choose the document type.** Is it a policy, an SOP, a work instruction, or a form? See [Policies, SOPs, and forms](/contributing/policies-vs-sops).
2. **Start from the template.** Use the [SOP template](/contributing/sop-template). Give the SOP a code in the form `SOP-AAA-###`, where `AAA` is the category code from the [SOP Library](/sops).
3. **Write it.** Name roles, not people. Take any amounts, limits, or approval levels from the approved Delegation of Authority or the relevant policy. Do not invent them in the SOP.
4. **Resolve every `[TBC]`.** An SOP cannot be approved while any `[TBC]` remains.
5. **Complete the [Before you publish](/contributing/before-you-publish) checklist.**
6. **Open a pull request** (see [How to edit](/contributing/how-to-edit)).
7. **Approve.** The approver reviews it against [Review and approval](/contributing/review-and-approval) and approves it.
8. **Publish.** On approval, the change is merged and the site updates automatically.
9. **Update the document.** Set the version number, the approval, and the revision history.
10. **Review it** at the end of its review cycle, or sooner if the related policy or regulations change.

## 5. Quality checks and approval points

- The document uses the current template and has a unique code.
- No `[TBC]` remains in an approved SOP.
- The approver is not the author.
- The revision history and version number are updated.
