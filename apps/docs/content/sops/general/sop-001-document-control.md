## 1. Purpose

Explain how SOPs at the GRIT Hub are written, reviewed, approved, and changed, so everyone follows the same process.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all SOPs published on this site and to everyone who proposes changes to them.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Author** | Drafts or updates the SOP and opens a pull request. |
| **SOP owner** | Accountable for the content being correct and current. |
| **Approver** | Reviews the pull request and approves it or asks for changes. |
| **Site maintainer** | Looks after the repository, navigation, and publishing. |

## 4. Procedure

1. Copy the [SOP template](/contributing/sop-template).
2. Write or update the SOP and fill in the document control table.
3. Complete the [Before you publish](/contributing/before-you-publish) checklist.
4. Raise the change for review (see [Review and approval](/contributing/review-and-approval)).
5. The approver reviews and approves the change.
6. On approval the change is merged and the site updates automatically.
7. Update the version number and revision history.
