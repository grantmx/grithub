## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how GRIT Hub receives, assesses, approves, and pays refund requests for courses, products, and coworking and group space bookings, in line with the [Refund Policy](/policies/refund-policy).

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

1. **Receive the request.** Requests must be in writing, sent to [workforce@grithub.org.za](mailto:workforce@grithub.org.za), with **proof of payment** and the **reason** for the refund. If anything is missing, ask the requester for it.
2. **Log it.** Record the date received, the requester, what was bought, the amount paid, and the reason in the refund register. [TBC: where the register is kept]
3. **Identify the type.** Is it a short course or workshop, a long-term or certification programme, an online or self-paced course, a physical product, a digital product, or a coworking or group space booking? Did GRIT Hub cancel?
4. **Apply the refund rules** in the table below.
5. **Consider special circumstances.** In exceptional cases, such as a medical emergency or unforeseen personal circumstances, the request may be reviewed case by case. Ask for supporting documents.
6. **Approve or decline.** The approver records the decision and the reason. [TBC: approval levels]
7. **Reply to the requester** with the decision. [TBC: timeframe for replying] Where it fits, GRIT Hub may offer an alternative such as a course credit or an exchange.
8. **Pay approved refunds** within **14 working days**, through the original payment method. Record the payment reference and include the refund in the regular reconciliation.

### Refund rules

| What was bought | Full refund | Partial refund | No refund |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Short course or workshop** | Cancelled at least 7 days before the start date | 50% if cancelled within 3 days of the start date | After the course has started, or the learner does not attend |
| **Long-term course or certification programme** | Withdrawn before the first session | Withdrawn within the first 2 weeks (a pro-rata refund may apply) | Withdrawn after 2 weeks. Deposits or registration fees may be non-refundable where stated. |
| **Online or self-paced course** | If a technical problem blocks access and is not fixed within 5 working days (may be granted) | None | Once the course materials have been accessed |
| **Physical product** | Returned within 14 days of purchase, original and unused. The customer pays return shipping unless the product is defective. | None | Damaged or used, or returned after 14 days |
| **Digital product** | If it is defective or inaccessible because of a GRIT Hub error (may be granted) | None | Once accessed, downloaded, or used |
| **Coworking desk or group space** | Cancelled at least 24 hours before arrival | None | Cancelled less than 24 hours before arrival |
| **Any booking** | Cancelled within 2 hours of venue confirmation | None | None |
| **Cancelled by GRIT Hub** | Always. For courses, the learner may choose a transfer to a future session instead. | None | None |

>
> **Open question for the policy owner.** The Refund Policy gives a full refund at least 7 days before a short course starts, and 50% within 3 days of the start. It does not say what happens between 3 and 7 days before the start. Decide the rule and update the policy and this table. [TBC]
