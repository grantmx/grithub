## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains what to do when someone is hurt, when something nearly goes wrong, or when a hazard is spotted at any GRIT Hub location. It applies to employees, learners, visitors, and stakeholders. It does not cover personal-information incidents (see [SOP-DAT-001](/sops/data-protection/sop-dat-001-personal-information-incidents-and-requests)) or safeguarding concerns ([TBC: link to the safeguarding procedure]).

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

1. **Make the situation safe.** Help anyone who is hurt. Give first aid. If there is a fire or immediate danger, evacuate using the marked routes and exits. Call emergency services when needed. Emergency contact numbers are displayed in visible places. [TBC: the list of numbers for each location]
2. **Report immediately** to the Safety Officer. Report accidents, injuries, near misses, and hazards. [TBC: how to reach the Safety Officer, including out of hours]
3. **Record it.** The Safety Officer records the date, time, place, who was involved, what happened, and any injuries or damage. [TBC: incident form and register]
4. **Investigate.** Find out what caused the incident and how to prevent it happening again.
5. **Agree corrective actions.** Record each action, who is responsible, and the due date. [TBC: how actions are tracked to completion]
6. **Keep the records.**
7. **Report externally where required.** [TBC: confirm which incidents must be reported to the Department of Employment and Labour or to other authorities, and by whom and by when]
8. **Review.** Look at incidents and near misses for patterns, and use them to update risk assessments, training, and drills. [TBC: how often]

## 5. Quality checks and approval points

- Every report is acknowledged and recorded.
- Every investigation ends with corrective actions that have owners and due dates.
- Fire drills and evacuation drills are held regularly. [TBC: frequency]
- All employees and learners receive health and safety training when they start, with refreshers.
