## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how facilitators communicate with learners through the LMS: announcements, discussion boards, direct messages, live sessions and section notes. All of it stays inside the LMS so it is on record and can be shown to the QCTO. Help requests are in [SOP-TRN-012 Learner Support](/sops/training/sop-trn-012-learner-support).

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Facilitator** (LMS role) | Posts announcements and notes, runs discussions and live sessions, records attendance, and answers messages for their cohorts. |
| **Assessor** (LMS role) | Answers direct messages from learners in their cohort. |
| **SDP admin** (LMS role) | Can act as a facilitator for any cohort; handles reported messages; exports the communication log; keeps the conferencing licence reference current. |
| **Learner** | Reads announcements, takes part in discussions and sessions, and follows the conduct rules. |

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

### Part A: Announcements

1. **Post an announcement** to the cohort for anything every learner must know (dates, venue changes, reminders). One announcement per cohort can be pinned to the top.
2. **Learners are notified** in the LMS and by email, as each learner chooses: straight away, in a weekly digest, or not at all. Emails never contain the message text, only a link to sign in and read it.

### Part B: Discussion boards

1. **Each module has a discussion board per cohort.** Only staff start threads. Learners reply, one level deep.
2. **Answer learner replies** within [TBC: target time]. Pin useful threads.
3. **Learners may edit their own reply for 15 minutes;** earlier text is kept on record. Nobody deletes posts.
4. **Moderate:** learners can report a post. The facilitator reviews reports and hides posts that break the conduct rules. A hidden post is kept for the record. [TBC: link to the learner code of conduct]

### Part C: Direct messages

1. **A learner can message one named facilitator of their cohort, or the cohort's assessor.** Only the two people in the conversation can read it.
2. **Reply within** [TBC: target time]. Keep messages about learning; move anything personal or sensitive to a support ticket or an in-person conversation.
3. **Reported messages** go to the SDP admins (not the facilitator in the conversation), who may hide them.

### Part D: Live sessions (Zoom)

1. **Schedule each live session** in the LMS with its Zoom meeting link (only Zoom links are accepted). Learners receive a calendar invitation by email, and another whenever the session changes or is cancelled. The platform is Zoom under GRIT Hub's subscription (4.2.8, 4.2.9).
2. **Record attendance** on the session's register: Present, Late, Absent or Excused.
3. **Add the recording link** after the session, if it was recorded. [TBC: rules for recording sessions and telling participants]

### Part E: Section notes

1. **Post notes for your cohort on a section** (4.2.10): clarifications, worked examples, or a link to a recording. Learners see them under the lesson as "From your facilitator". The Section notes screen shows which sections have no notes yet.

### Part F: Evidence

1. **The communication log** (announcements, discussions, messages, tickets, sessions, attendance and notes) is exported per cohort and date range by an SDP admin or an external evaluator, as evidence for Form 5. Every export is recorded with a fingerprint of the file.

> [!WARNING]
>
> Don't use personal WhatsApp groups, personal email or social media for course communication. It can't be kept on record or shown to the QCTO, and it exposes learners' phone numbers. [TBC: confirm as policy]

## 6. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A post or message is abusive or unsafe | Hide it, then escalate to the process owner. [TBC: safeguarding route] |
| Zoom is unavailable | Reschedule in the LMS; learners get the updated invitation automatically. |
| A learner shares personal information in a public thread | Hide the post and remind the learner; tell the Information Officer if it concerns someone else. |
