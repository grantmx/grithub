## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how assessments are prepared, marked, moderated, recorded, and reported, so that results are fair, valid, and reliable. It applies to all GRIT Hub training programmes, including QCTO-accredited occupational qualifications.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Curriculum Development and Qualifications Director** | Oversees quality assurance and consistency with QCTO frameworks. |
| **Facilitators and assessors** | Prepare, conduct, and mark assessments, and give feedback. |
| **Internal moderators** | Check assessments for fairness, validity, and reliability. Appointed internally and rotated. |
| **Admin staff** | Keep accurate records of registrations, outcomes, and moderation. |
| **QCTO-appointed external moderators** | Review assessment and moderation records as required by QCTO. |
| **Learners** | Complete assessments honestly and may ask for clarification of results. |

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

1. **Prepare the assessment.** Choose the type: formative, summative, practical, or self and peer. Check that it measures the learning outcomes and, for QCTO programmes, the occupational outcomes. Use criteria-based marking.
2. **Tell learners in advance.** Before the assessment, tell learners the criteria, the grading method, the grade thresholds, and how feedback will be given.
3. **Arrange special considerations.** Provide reasonable accommodations for learners with disabilities or special needs. Learners with extenuating circumstances, such as illness or a personal emergency, may request an extension or an alternative assessment. [TBC: who approves, and the deadline for requests]
4. **Conduct the assessment.** Work must be original and properly referenced. Plagiarism detection tools are used where necessary.
5. **Deal with dishonesty.** Plagiarism, cheating, or other academic dishonesty leads to disciplinary action. [TBC: link to the disciplinary procedure]
6. **Mark the assessment** against the published criteria.
7. **Moderate internally.** All assessments are moderated. The moderator is appointed internally and rotated for objectivity. Sampling follows QCTO moderation requirements. [TBC: sample size or rate, and who selects the sample] Record the result in a moderation report.
8. **Release results and give feedback.** Feedback is timely and covers strengths, areas for improvement, and suggested learning resources. [TBC: number of working days for feedback] [TBC: confirm that results are released only after internal moderation]
9. **Offer reassessment where applicable.** A learner who fails may be given a second opportunity to retake or improve their work. The trainer or academic committee decides the criteria. [TBC: composition of the academic committee, the number of attempts allowed, and the timeframe]
10. **Refer disputes to appeals.** A learner who believes the assessment was unfairly graded may appeal. Follow [SOP-TRN-005 Appeals Handling](/sops/training/sop-trn-005-appeals-handling).
11. **Keep the records** (see section 4).
12. **Support external moderation.** Work with QCTO-appointed moderators within the regulatory timelines, and provide the assessment and moderation records they ask for.

- Every assessment is internally moderated, and moderators are rotated.
- Learners received the criteria and thresholds before the assessment.
- Feedback was given within the agreed time. [TBC]
- Records are complete before any external moderation visit.
