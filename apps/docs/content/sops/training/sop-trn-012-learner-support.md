## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how learners ask for help through the LMS and how staff respond, on time and on record. It covers academic questions, technical problems and administrative requests. Day-to-day teaching communication is in [SOP-TRN-013 Facilitator Communication](/sops/training/sop-trn-013-facilitator-communication).

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Learner** | Asks for help through the Help section of Messages, and replies to staff questions. |
| **Facilitator** (LMS role) | Answers academic tickets for their cohorts; watches for learners who may be falling behind. |
| **SDP admin** (LMS role) | Answers technical and administrative tickets; reassigns tickets; follows up overdue ones. |
| **Process owner** | Reviews response-time figures and acts on trends. |

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

### Part A: A learner asks for help

1. **The learner opens a support ticket** (Messages → Help), chooses a category and describes the problem. Tickets don't accept attachments, so ID documents and other files stay out of support conversations.
2. **The LMS routes it:** academic tickets go to the cohort's facilitators; technical and administrative tickets go to the SDP admins.

### Part B: Staff respond

1. **First reply within 1 working day; resolve within 5 working days.** Working days are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 South African time, excluding public holidays. [TBC: confirm these targets in policy (they are the LMS defaults)]
2. **Update the status** as work progresses: Open → In progress → Resolved. Staff may reassign a ticket to another team member who can help.
3. **Overdue tickets** are flagged on the staff ticket list and the staff menu, and a daily email lists overdue counts. The SDP admin follows up every overdue ticket. [TBC: who covers when the assigned person is away]
4. **The learner can reopen** a resolved ticket if the problem isn't fixed. Staff close it when it is finished; a closed ticket can't be reopened.

### Part C: Other academic support

1. **Digital library:** each qualification has a library of extra readings, links and files ([SOP-TRN-008](/sops/training/sop-trn-008-knowledge-module-delivery)).
2. **Learners who may be falling behind:** staff see "at risk" flags when a learner has had no learning activity for a week, or has got every check try wrong in two sections within two weeks. These flags are for staff only and are never shown to the learner. The facilitator contacts the learner. [TBC: how quickly, and by which channel]
3. **Connectivity and devices:** learners who can't study online can use hub computers. [TBC: hub access hours and booking]

### Part D: Review

1. **Monthly,** the process owner reviews the on-time rates for first replies and resolutions (shown for the last 90 days on the staff ticket list) and records any actions. [TBC: confirm monthly]

> [!WARNING]
>
> Never ask a learner to send an ID number, password or sign-in link in a ticket or message. Identity checks follow [SOP-TRN-007](/sops/training/sop-trn-007-learner-invitation-and-administration).
