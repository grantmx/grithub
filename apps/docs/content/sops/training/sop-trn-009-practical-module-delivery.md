## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how the Practical module of a qualification is set up and delivered on the LMS: practical briefs, how learners submit evidence, and observed tasks at the hub. Marking and moderation of practical work are in [SOP-TRN-011](/sops/training/sop-trn-011-assessment-and-moderation-in-the-lms).

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Process owner** | Confirms the briefs cover every practical skill in the curriculum document. |
| **SDP admin** (LMS role) | Writes, publishes and schedules practical briefs. |
| **Facilitator** (LMS role) | Prepares learners, runs practical sessions, takes attendance and ID checks on assessment days. Does not open learners' submitted evidence. |
| **Assessor** (LMS role) | Starts and marks observed tasks; reviews submitted evidence. |
| **Learner** | Completes the task in their assigned version of the brief and submits evidence. |

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

### Part A: Set up practical briefs

1. **Write the brief** (SDP admin) for each practical module: instructions, the criteria (each judged Met or Not yet met), and the evidence expected. Map the brief to its curriculum component.
2. **Write equivalent versions (variants)** of the brief where copying is a risk. Every variant uses the same criteria.
3. **Have the brief reviewed** by [TBC: role, for example the assessor or process owner] before publishing.
4. **Publish the brief.** Once published, its criteria and variants are locked; only the title can change. A change means a new brief.
5. **Schedule it** for the cohort, as a submitted task (with a window) or an observed task (on an assessment day).
6. **Check readiness** (process owner): dashboard rows 4.3.1 and 4.3.3 are Ready (every practical module has a published brief with criteria). Row 4.3.2 is covered by the learner guide ([SOP-TRN-008](/sops/training/sop-trn-008-knowledge-module-delivery)).

### Part B: Learners complete submitted tasks

1. **Each learner gets one variant,** chosen at random but fixed for that learner: they always see the same version.
2. **The learner adds evidence while the submission is a draft:** files uploaded straight to private storage, or links to work on an approved service (for example a video host or code repository). The LMS accepts only agreed file types and sizes, checks the file content matches its type, and removes anything that doesn't. [TBC: confirm the accepted types and size limit in the Assessment Policy (LMS settings: PDF, images, Office files, text and ZIP; 20 MB per file; up to 10 items)]
3. **The learner submits.** Submitting is final: the evidence is frozen and can't be deleted.

### Part C: Observed tasks on assessment days

1. **Attendance and ID check** (facilitator or assessor) on the assessment-day register.
2. **The assessor starts the observation** for each learner and records the judgement against each criterion, with notes.
3. **The assessor signs off the register** at the end of the day. A signed-off register can't be changed.

>
> Uploaded files are not virus-scanned yet; the LMS labels them "not scanned". Staff open evidence only through the LMS, never by downloading and forwarding it. [TBC: connect a scanner]
