## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how the Knowledge module of a qualification is loaded onto the LMS, delivered to learners, and kept complete against the curriculum document. Assessments are covered in [SOP-TRN-010](/sops/training/sop-trn-010-question-bank-development-and-randomization) and [SOP-TRN-011](/sops/training/sop-trn-011-assessment-and-moderation-in-the-lms).

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

### Part A: Load the module

1. **Build the structure** (SDP admin): qualification → Knowledge module → sections, one section per curriculum topic or a logical part of one. Record the curriculum component code for each section.
2. **Write each section** (content author). Text comes first, so learners on low data can study. Content uses the LMS's Markdown format. Every image needs a text description (alt text).
3. **Add audio or video only where it helps.** Media is hosted by the LMS's video provider and never plays automatically. Every published audio or video item needs a full transcript.
4. **Add one check question per section** ([SOP-TRN-010](/sops/training/sop-trn-010-question-bank-development-and-randomization)).
5. **Upload the learner guide** (SDP admin, Form 5 readiness dashboard): one PDF per qualification. It is the evidence that every topic in the curriculum document is covered (4.2.2). The LMS checks that it is a real PDF and records its fingerprint.
6. **Publish library resources** (SDP admin): extra readings, links and files for academic support (4.2.7).
7. **Check readiness** (process owner): on the dashboard, rows 4.2.1, 4.2.2, 4.2.3 and 4.2.7 show Ready. The dashboard flags any knowledge section without published text or without a check question.

### Part B: Deliver

1. **Learners study online, at their own pace,** within the cohort's dates. Each section shows the text, any media, notes from the facilitator, and the check question.
2. **Section check:** the learner must answer the check question (right or wrong) before marking the section complete. They see the correct answer and explanation after answering. A retry asks the same question with the options in a new order. [TBC: confirm the number of tries (the LMS allows 3)]
3. **Progress:** the LMS records completed sections and where the learner stopped reading, so they can continue on any device.
4. **Facilitator notes** (4.2.10): facilitators post notes for their cohort per section, optionally with a recording link. The Section notes screen lists every section, so gaps are easy to see.
5. **Formative assessments** for the module appear on the programme page within their windows ([SOP-TRN-011](/sops/training/sop-trn-011-assessment-and-moderation-in-the-lms)).
6. **Watch progress** (facilitator): use the progress report to find learners who have stopped, and contact them ([SOP-TRN-012](/sops/training/sop-trn-012-learner-support)).

### Part C: Change content

1. **Correct or improve a section** (content author), and tell the process owner if the change affects curriculum coverage.
2. **When the curriculum document changes,** the process owner reviews every section, the learner guide and the question banks, and uploads a new learner guide version.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A topic in the curriculum document has no content | Process owner assigns it to a content author before the cohort reaches it. |
| Media won't play for learners | Learners use the transcript; the facilitator reports it to the technical custodian. |
| A learner can't study online (no device or data) | Book hub computer time. [TBC: hub access arrangements] |
