## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP has two parts. **Part A** explains what to do when personal information may have been lost, stolen, or seen by someone who should not see it. **Part B** explains how to handle requests from people about their own personal information. It applies to all employees, learners, partners, and others who handle personal information for GRIT Hub.

## 3. Step-by-step operating procedure

### Part A: Suspected or confirmed breach

1. **Report immediately** to the Information Officer. Say what happened, when, what information was involved, and who may be affected. [TBC: reporting channel, including an out-of-hours contact]
2. **Contain it.** Stop the loss or exposure as quickly as you can. [TBC: standard containment steps, for example removing access or recovering a device]
3. **Log it.** The Information Officer records the incident in the incident log. [TBC: where the log is kept]
4. **Assess the impact.** What information was involved, how many people are affected, and what harm could result?
5. **Take corrective action** to fix the cause and stop it happening again.
6. **Notify where required.** Affected data subjects and the Information Regulator are notified where required. [TBC: confirm when notification is required, how soon, and who signs it off]
7. **Close the incident.** Record the outcome and the lessons learned, and update controls or training where needed.

### Part B: Requests from data subjects

A data subject may ask to access their personal information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its processing, or to withdraw consent where applicable.

1. **Receive the request** and pass it to the Information Officer. [TBC: channel]
2. **Confirm the identity** of the person asking. [TBC: acceptable proof of identity]
3. **Log the request** with the date received and the type of request.
4. **Find the information** across all the places it is held, including learner records and digital storage.
5. **Respond.** Provide the information, or carry out the correction, deletion, or stop in processing, unless there is a legal reason not to. Record any such reason. [TBC: response deadline]
6. **Confirm in writing** to the person what was done.
7. **Handle complaints.** The Information Officer handles complaints about the handling of personal information and liaises with the Information Regulator where necessary.
