# GRIT Hub Policies and SOPs

These are the policies and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) of the GRIT Hub, the Garden Route Innovation and Technology Hub. They are published openly so that other hubs can learn from them, copy them, and adapt them.

## How the documents fit together

- **[Policies](/policies)** set out what we require and why.
- **[SOPs](/sops)** explain step by step how each activity is carried out, who does it, and what evidence we keep.

Every SOP links to the policy behind it. See [Policies, SOPs, and forms](/contributing/policies-vs-sops) for the difference.

## Where to start

- **Find a document:** browse the [Policy Library](/policies) or the [SOP Library](/sops), or use the search box.
- **Reuse a document at your own hub:** read [Use these documents](/reuse).
- **Suggest a change or add an SOP:** read [How to edit](/contributing/how-to-edit), then start from the [SOP template](/contributing/sop-template).
- **New to Markdown?** The [cheat sheet](/getting-started/markdown-cheatsheet) takes about 10 minutes.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Always check the version and review date of a document. Printed or downloaded copies may be out of date.
