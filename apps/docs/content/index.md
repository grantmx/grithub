# GRIT Hub SOPs

These are the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) of the GRIT Hub, the Garden Route Innovation and Technology Hub. They are published openly so that other hubs can learn from them, copy them, and adapt them.

## Where to start

- **Find a procedure:** browse the [SOP Library](/sops) or use the search box.
- **Reuse an SOP at your own hub:** read [Use these SOPs](/reuse).
- **Suggest a change or add an SOP:** read [How to edit](/contributing/how-to-edit), then start from the [SOP template](/contributing/sop-template).
- **New to Markdown?** The [cheat sheet](/getting-started/markdown-cheatsheet) takes about 10 minutes.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Always check the version and review date at the top of an SOP. Printed or downloaded copies may be out of date.
