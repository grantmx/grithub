We share our policies and SOPs so that more innovation and technology hubs can start from something that already works, instead of from a blank page.

Add a line like this to anything you adapt:

```text

```

## How to adapt a document for your hub

1. Copy the text into your own document or site.
2. Replace names, roles, forms, systems, amounts, deadlines, and contact details with your own.
3. Check every legal, safety, and regulatory reference against the rules where you operate. These documents were written for a hub in South Africa.
4. Take thresholds and approval levels from your own governing documents. Do not copy ours.
5. Have the person who will own the document read it and confirm that it matches how you really work.
6. Set your own version number, owner, approval, and review date.

> [!WARNING]
>
> These documents are provided as they are, as examples. They are not legal, financial, or safety advice. You are responsible for making sure that your version is correct and suitable for your hub.
