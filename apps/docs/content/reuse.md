We share these SOPs so that more innovation and technology hubs can start from something that already works, instead of from a blank page.

## How to adapt an SOP for your hub

1. Copy the SOP text into your own document or site.
2. Replace names, roles, forms, systems, and contact details with your own.
3. Check every legal, safety, and regulatory reference against the rules where you operate. These SOPs were written for a hub in South Africa.
4. Have the person who will own the SOP read it and confirm that it matches how you really work.
5. Set your own version number, owner, and review date.

> [!WARNING]
>
> These SOPs are provided as they are, as examples. They are not legal, financial, or safety advice. You are responsible for making sure that your version is correct and suitable for your hub.
