## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy outlines the refund terms and conditions for courses, training programs, products, and coworking space bookings offered by the Garden Route Innovation & Technology Hub (GRIT Hub). It ensures clarity and fairness in processing refund requests while maintaining the integrity of GRIT Hub’s educational and business offerings.

## 2. General Refund Principles

- Refunds shall be processed in accordance with this policy to ensure fairness and transparency.
- Refund requests must be submitted in writing within the specified timeframes.
- Refund eligibility is determined based on the type of course, product, or coworking space booking.
- Non-refundable fees may apply where indicated.

## 3. Course Refund Policy

### 3.1 Short Courses and Workshops

- **Full Refund:** If a learner cancels enrollment at least **7 days before** the course start date.
- **50% Refund:** If a learner cancels enrollment **within 3 days** of the course start date.
- **No Refund:** If cancellation occurs **after the course has started** or the learner fails to attend.
- If GRIT Hub cancels a course, learners shall receive a **full refund** or the option to transfer to a future session.

### 3.2 Long-Term Courses and Certification Programs

- **Full Refund:** If a learner withdraws **before the first session** of the course.
- **Partial Refund:** If a learner withdraws **within the first 2 weeks** of the course (a pro-rata refund may apply).
- **No Refund:** If withdrawal occurs **after 2 weeks** from the course start date.
- Course deposits or registration fees may be **non-refundable** where stated.

### 3.3 Online and Self-Paced Courses

- **No Refunds** once course materials have been accessed.
- If a technical issue prevents course access and is not resolved within **5 working days**, a **full refund** may be granted.

## 4. Product Refund Policy

### 4.1 Physical Products (e.g., Training Materials, Merchandise)

- **Full Refund:** If returned **within 14 days** of purchase in its original, unused condition.
- **No Refund:** If the product is damaged, used, or returned after **14 days** from purchase.
- Customers must cover return shipping costs unless the product is defective.

### 4.2 Digital Products (e.g., E-books, Software, Online Content)

- **No Refunds** once a digital product has been accessed, downloaded, or used.
- Refunds may be granted if the product is defective or inaccessible due to a GRIT Hub error.

## 5. Coworking & Group Space Refund Policy

### 5.1 Refund Policy

- In the unlikely event that GRIT Hub needs to cancel your booking, a **full refund** will be issued.

### 5.2 Cancellation Policy

- **All Bookings:** Can be canceled within **2 hours** of venue confirmation for a full refund.
- **Desks:** Cancellations must be made **at least 24 hours** before the arrival time to receive a refund.
- **Group Spaces:** Cancellations must be made **at least 24 hours** before the arrival time to receive a refund.

## 6. Refund Process

- Refund requests must be submitted in writing to [workforce@grithub.org.za](mailto:workforce@grithub.org.za) with proof of payment and reason for refund.
- Approved refunds shall be processed within **14 working days** via the original payment method.
- GRIT Hub reserves the right to offer alternative solutions (such as course credits or exchanges) where applicable.

## 7. Special Circumstances

- In exceptional cases (e.g., medical emergencies, unforeseen personal circumstances), refund requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
- Supporting documentation may be required for special refund considerations.

## 8. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually or as necessary based on business and regulatory requirements.
- Amendments must be approved by the Executive Team.
