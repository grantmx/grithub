## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy outlines the principles, standards, and procedures for training, teaching, and learning at the Garden Route Innovation & Technology Hub (GRIT Hub). It aims to ensure high-quality educational experiences, inclusivity, and alignment with industry and regulatory standards while fostering innovation and practical skill development.

## 2. Guiding Principles

- **Quality and Excellence:** Training programs shall meet high standards of instructional design, content relevance, and learning outcomes.
- **Inclusivity and Accessibility:** Learning opportunities shall be open to individuals from diverse backgrounds, including underrepresented groups.
- **Industry Alignment:** Programs shall be aligned with current industry trends and workforce needs to enhance employability.
- **Experiential Learning:** A practical, hands-on approach shall be prioritized to reinforce theoretical concepts.
- **Ethical and Responsible Learning:** All training shall adhere to ethical standards, including intellectual property respect and responsible technology use.

## 3. Training Programs and Curriculum

### 3.1 Course Development

- Training programs shall be designed based on industry needs, technological advancements, and input from subject matter experts.
- Programs shall align with national and international qualifications frameworks where applicable (e.g., QCTO, SAQA, MICT SETA).
- Course objectives, syllabi, and assessment methods shall be clearly defined and reviewed periodically.

### 3.2 Teaching Methodologies

- A blended learning approach, combining online and in-person training, shall be encouraged.
- Active learning strategies, such as project-based learning, case studies, and peer collaboration, shall be integrated.
- Digital learning tools and platforms shall be utilized to enhance engagement and accessibility.

### 3.3 Trainer Qualifications

- Trainers must possess relevant industry experience, teaching credentials, and a commitment to continuous professional development.
- Periodic trainer evaluations and professional development sessions shall be conducted.

### 3.4 Learner Support

- GRIT Hub shall provide mentorship, career guidance, and academic support for learners.
- Special accommodations shall be available for learners with disabilities or specific learning needs.

## 4. Assessment and Certification

### 4.1 Assessment Standards

- Assessments shall be fair, transparent, and aligned with learning outcomes.
- A combination of formative (ongoing) and summative (final) assessments shall be used.

### 4.2 Certification

- Learners who successfully complete a course shall receive a GRIT Hub certificate of completion.
- Where applicable, certifications shall be co-issued with accredited partners.

## 5. Training Ethics and Conduct

### 5.1 Code of Conduct

- All trainers and learners shall adhere to a code of conduct promoting professionalism, respect, and academic integrity.
- Plagiarism, cheating, and other forms of academic dishonesty shall not be tolerated.

### 5.2 Data Protection and Privacy

- Learner and trainer information shall be protected in compliance with data protection regulations.
- Digital training materials shall be securely stored and accessed only by authorized personnel.

## 6. Monitoring and Evaluation

### 6.1 Continuous Improvement

- Training programs shall be periodically reviewed based on feedback from learners, trainers, and industry stakeholders.
- Performance metrics, such as learner success rates and post-training employment, shall be tracked to measure program impact.

### 6.2 Feedback Mechanisms

- Regular surveys, interviews, and engagement sessions shall be conducted to gather learner and trainer feedback.
- An open-door policy shall be encouraged for learners to express concerns or suggestions.

## 7. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually to ensure relevance and effectiveness.
- Amendments must be approved by the Executive Team and the Board of Directors.
