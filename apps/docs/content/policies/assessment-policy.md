## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for assessments at GRIT Hub, ensuring fair, transparent, and competency-based evaluation of learners. It applies to all training programs, courses, and skills development initiatives offered by GRIT Hub.

## 2. Principles of Assessment

- **Fairness:** Assessments shall be unbiased and provide equal opportunities for all learners.
- **Validity:** Assessments shall measure what they intend to measure, aligning with course learning outcomes.
- **Reliability:** Assessment results shall be consistent and reproducible across different learners and contexts.
- **Transparency:** Learners shall be informed about assessment criteria, grading methods, and feedback processes.
- **Practical Relevance:** Assessments shall incorporate real-world applications, emphasizing skills needed in industry and entrepreneurship.

## 3. Types of Assessments

### 3.1 Formative Assessments

- Conducted throughout the course to monitor learner progress.
- Includes quizzes, assignments, participation, peer reviews, and practical exercises.
- Feedback is provided to help learners improve continuously.

### 3.2 Summative Assessments

- Conducted at the end of a course/module to evaluate overall learning outcomes.
- Includes final exams, capstone projects, presentations, and portfolio reviews.
- Determines whether learners meet the competency requirements.

### 3.3 Practical Assessments

- Involves hands-on, project-based evaluations reflecting industry practices.
- Includes coding challenges, prototype development, business model pitches, and case studies.

### 3.4 Self and Peer Assessments

- Encourages learners to reflect on their progress and provide constructive feedback to peers.
- Helps develop critical thinking, collaboration, and problem-solving skills.

## 4. Grading and Feedback

### 4.1 Grading System

- GRIT Hub shall use a structured grading system appropriate to each course.
- Pass/fail, percentage-based, or competency-based grading may be used depending on the program.
- Specific grade thresholds shall be communicated before assessments.

### 4.2 Feedback Mechanism

- Timely feedback shall be provided after each assessment.
- Feedback shall include strengths, areas for improvement, and suggested learning resources.
- Learners shall have the right to seek clarification on their grades.

## 5. Assessment Integrity and Security

### 5.1 Plagiarism and Academic Honesty

- All submitted work must be original and properly referenced.
- Plagiarism, cheating, or any form of academic dishonesty shall result in disciplinary action.
- GRIT Hub shall use plagiarism detection tools where necessary.

### 5.2 Confidentiality and Data Protection

- Assessment data shall be securely stored and only accessible to authorized personnel.
- Learner performance records shall be handled in compliance with data protection regulations.

## 6. Reassessment and Appeals

Refer to our official [Appeals Policy](/policies/appeals-policy) for the full details.

### 6.1 Reassessment Policy

- Learners who fail an assessment may be given a second opportunity to retake or improve their submission.
- Reassessment criteria shall be determined by the trainer or academic committee.

### 6.2 Appeals Process

- Learners who believe their assessment was unfairly graded may submit a formal appeal.
- Appeals must be submitted in writing within a specified period after results are released.
- The appeal shall be reviewed by an independent panel, and a final decision shall be communicated.

## 7. Special Considerations

- Learners facing extenuating circumstances (e.g., illness, personal emergencies) may request extensions or alternative assessments.
- Reasonable accommodations shall be provided for learners with disabilities or special needs.

## 8. Continuous Improvement

- Assessment policies shall be periodically reviewed based on learner feedback, industry trends, and educational best practices.
- Trainers shall receive ongoing training to improve assessment design and implementation.

## 9. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually to ensure alignment with best practices and regulatory requirements.
- Amendments must be approved by the Executive Team and the Board of Directors.
