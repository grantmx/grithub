## 1. Quality Assurance Policy

### Purpose

This policy affirms GRIT Hub’s commitment to delivering quality in all facets of QCTO-accredited program implementation.

### Policy Statement

GRIT Hub ensures that all occupational qualifications are delivered and assessed according to the highest standards of quality, integrity, and accountability, in line with QCTO regulations and national qualifications standards.

### Roles and Responsibilities

- **Curriculum Development & Qualifications Director**: Oversees system-wide implementation of QA processes and Ensure consistency and compliance with QCTO frameworks.
- **Facilitators and Assessors**: Uphold standards in delivery and assessment.
- **Admin Staff**: Support accurate record-keeping and documentation.

## 2. Teaching and Learning Strategy

### Instructional Methodologies

- Outcomes-based education aligned to the Occupational Qualifications Framework (OQF).
- Blended learning (face-to-face and digital) where appropriate.
- Use of real-life occupational tasks in simulations or workplace-based settings.

### Learning Support Systems

- Learning Management System (LMS) for content access and communication.
- Scheduled learner support sessions (academic and technical).

### Facilitator Responsibilities

- Prepare and deliver sessions aligned with occupational profiles.
- Provide timely feedback and support to learners.
- Maintain continuous professional development and alignment with curriculum updates

## 3. Assessment Policy

(Refer to the existing [Assessment Policy](/policies/assessment-policy) for full details.)

- Fair and transparent formative and summative assessments.
- Criteria-based marking aligned to occupational outcomes.
- Internal moderation and verification of results.
- Appeals and grievance procedures.

## 4. Moderation Policy

### Internal Moderation

- Moderation conducted for all assessments to ensure fairness, validity, and reliability.
- Moderators appointed internally and rotated to ensure objectivity.
- Sampling of assessments per QCTO moderation requirements.

### External Moderation

- Engagement with QCTO-appointed moderators as per regulatory timelines.
- Provision of assessment and moderation records for external review.

## 5. Learner Support Policy

### Support Mechanisms

- Academic tutoring and mentorship.
- Psychosocial support via partnerships or referrals.
- Technical support for digital learning platforms.

### Complaints and Feedback

- Clearly communicated grievance procedures.
- Anonymous feedback channels.
- Prompt resolution and learner communication follow-up.

## 6. Facilitator Recruitment and Development Policy

### Recruitment Criteria

- Minimum qualifications aligned with occupational subject matter.
- Prior teaching, training, or industry experience in the relevant field.
- Registration with relevant professional bodies where applicable.

### Professional Development

- Ongoing CPD workshops, peer learning, and curriculum review participation.
- Annual performance appraisals and classroom observations.
- Access to latest QCTO and industry-aligned teaching resources.

## 7. Workplace-Based Learning (WBL) Policy (if applicable)

### Integration with Program Delivery

- Structured WBL as part of curriculum where required.
- Signed Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs) with host employers.

### Monitoring and Support

- Learner logbooks to track on-the-job learning outcomes.
- Assigned workplace mentors and GRIT Hub workplace supervisors.
- Mid-placement and final evaluations of learner progress.

## 8. Data Management and Record-Keeping Policy

### Record-Keeping Standards

- Secure and confidential storage of:
  - Learner registration details
  - Assessment outcomes
  - Moderation reports
  - Certification records

### Compliance

- Full compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
- Access rights and data retention periods are clearly defined.
- Regular backups and secure digital storage.

## 9. Certification Policy

(Refer to the existing [Certification Policy](/policies/certification-policy) for full details.)

- Clear process for awarding and issuing certificates.
- Verification of achievement before certification.
- Revocation process in the case of fraudulent activity.

## 10. Monitoring, Evaluation, and Review Policy

### Continuous Improvement Framework

- Annual program review to align with latest QCTO standards and industry trends.
- Regular stakeholder consultation, including learners, employers, and facilitators.

### Evaluation Tools

- End-of-program surveys and focus groups.
- Tracer studies to measure employment and progression of graduates.
- Review and update of policies and curricula based on feedback and data analysis.
