## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the commitment of the Garden Route Innovation & Technology Hub (GRIT Hub) to providing a safe and healthy working and learning environment for employees, learners, visitors, and stakeholders. It ensures compliance with the **Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA) of South Africa** and other relevant regulations.

## 2. Health and Safety Principles

- **Safety First:** All activities at GRIT Hub shall prioritize the health and safety of individuals.
- **Compliance:** Adherence to all applicable occupational health and safety laws and regulations.
- **Prevention:** Proactive identification, assessment, and mitigation of workplace hazards.
- **Responsibility:** Shared accountability for safety among management, employees, and learners.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Regular evaluation and enhancement of safety measures.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Management Responsibilities

- Ensure compliance with OHS laws and workplace safety regulations.
- Implement and maintain safety policies, procedures, and training programs.
- Conduct regular risk assessments to identify and mitigate hazards.
- Provide necessary safety equipment and emergency response resources.

### 3.2 Employee and Learner Responsibilities

- Follow all OHS policies, procedures, and safety instructions.
- Report any hazards, accidents, or near misses to the designated safety officer.
- Participate in health and safety training and emergency drills.
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE) where required.

### 3.3 Safety Officer

- Oversee and implement OHS measures.
- Conduct regular workplace inspections and audits.
- Investigate incidents and recommend corrective actions.
- Liaise with regulatory bodies and ensure compliance with safety standards.

## 4. Hazard Identification and Risk Management

- GRIT Hub shall conduct regular safety risk assessments to identify potential hazards.
- Hazards shall be categorized into:
  - **Physical hazards** (e.g., slips, trips, falls, electrical risks).
  - **Chemical hazards** (e.g., exposure to hazardous substances).
  - **Ergonomic hazards** (e.g., improper workstation setup).
  - **Biological hazards** (e.g., exposure to infectious agents).
- Control measures shall be implemented to eliminate or reduce risks.

## 5. Emergency Preparedness and Response

### 5.1 Fire Safety

- Fire extinguishers shall be strategically placed and regularly maintained.
- Fire evacuation routes and emergency exits shall be clearly marked.
- Fire drills shall be conducted periodically.

### 5.2 First Aid and Medical Response

- A first aid kit shall be available at all GRIT Hub locations.
- At least one staff member shall be trained in first aid and CPR.
- Emergency contact numbers shall be displayed in visible locations.

### 5.3 Evacuation Procedures

- All personnel shall be trained on emergency evacuation procedures.
- Regular emergency drills shall be conducted to ensure preparedness.

## 6. Health and Wellness

- GRIT Hub promotes a healthy work environment by encouraging good ergonomics, mental health awareness, and wellness initiatives.
- Reasonable accommodations shall be provided for individuals with disabilities or medical conditions.

## 7. Incident Reporting and Investigation

- All accidents, injuries, and near misses must be reported immediately to the Safety Officer.
- An incident investigation shall be conducted to determine the cause and prevent recurrence.
- Corrective actions shall be implemented, and records shall be maintained.

## 8. Training and Awareness

- All employees and learners shall receive OHS training during onboarding.
- Specialized safety training shall be provided for high-risk tasks.
- Regular refresher training sessions shall be conducted.

## 9. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually or as required by regulatory updates.
- Amendments must be approved by the Board of Directors.
