## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy outlines the Garden Route Innovation & Technology Hub (GRIT Hub) commitment to compliance with the **Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa** to ensure the lawful processing, collection, storage, and protection of personal information. It applies to all employees, learners, partners, and stakeholders interacting with GRIT Hub.

## 2. Principles of Data Protection

GRIT Hub adheres to the following POPIA principles:

- **Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency:** Personal data shall be processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner.
- **Purpose Limitation:** Data shall only be collected for a specific, explicit, and legitimate purpose.
- **Data Minimization:** Only necessary data shall be collected and processed.
- **Accuracy:** Reasonable steps shall be taken to ensure personal data is accurate and up to date.
- **Storage Limitation:** Data shall not be retained for longer than required for its intended purpose.
- **Integrity and Confidentiality:** Adequate security measures shall be in place to protect personal data.

## 3. Processing of Personal Information

### 3.1 Collection of Personal Information

- Personal information shall only be collected with consent or as required by law.
- The purpose of data collection shall be clearly communicated to the data subject.
- Data may include names, contact details, identification numbers, financial information, and employment details.

### 3.2 Use and Sharing of Personal Information

- Personal data shall only be used for the stated purpose at the time of collection.
- Data shall not be shared with third parties without the consent of the data subject, except where legally required.
- Third-party service providers handling personal data must comply with POPIA requirements.

### 3.3 Data Subject Rights

- Data subjects have the right to:
  - Access their personal data held by GRIT Hub.
  - Request correction or deletion of incorrect or unnecessary data.
  - Object to the processing of their personal data.
  - Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable.

## 4. Data Security and Protection

### 4.1 Security Measures

- GRIT Hub shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
- Access to personal data shall be restricted to authorized personnel.
- Data encryption and secure storage shall be used where necessary.

### 4.2 Data Breach Management

- Any suspected or confirmed data breach shall be reported immediately to the designated **Information Officer**.
- GRIT Hub shall assess the impact of the breach and take corrective action.
- Affected data subjects and the Information Regulator shall be notified where required.

## 5. Information Officer

- The **Information Officer** shall oversee compliance with POPIA and manage data protection policies.
- Responsibilities include:
  - Ensuring staff and stakeholders are trained on POPIA compliance.
  - Handling data subject requests and complaints.
  - Liaising with the Information Regulator where necessary.

## 6. Record Retention and Disposal

- Personal data shall only be retained for as long as necessary for business or legal purposes.
- Secure disposal methods shall be used for outdated or unnecessary records.

## 7. Compliance and Enforcement

- All employees, learners, and stakeholders handling personal data must comply with this policy.
- Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, legal consequences, or other corrective measures.

## 8. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually or as required by regulatory updates.
- Amendments must be approved by the Board of Directors.
