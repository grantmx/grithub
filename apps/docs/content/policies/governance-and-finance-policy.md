## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the governance and financial management framework for the Garden Route Innovation & Technology Hub (GRIT Hub). It ensures transparency, accountability, and compliance with legal and regulatory requirements while promoting financial sustainability and ethical decision-making.

## 2. Governance Structure

### 2.1 Board of Directors

- The Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the strategic direction and financial health of GRIT Hub.
- The Board shall comprise of a minimum of three (3) members.
- Members shall be appointed for a term of three (3) years, with the possibility of renewal.
- The Board shall meet at least once per year to review operations, financial performance, and strategic initiatives.

### 2.2 Executive Leadership

- The Executive Director (or CEO) is responsible for day-to-day operations, financial management, and executing Board-approved strategies.
- The Executive Team includes key department heads responsible for program implementation, compliance, and financial management.

### 2.3 Advisory Committees

- GRIT Hub may establish advisory committees, such as a Finance Committee, Audit Committee, and Ethics Committee, to provide expert guidance on governance and financial matters.

## 3. Financial Management

### 3.1 Budgeting and Financial Planning

- The Board shall approve an annual budget outlining projected income and expenditure.
- A financial plan will be developed to ensure long-term sustainability and growth.

### 3.2 Financial Controls

- All financial transactions must comply with applicable accounting standards and regulatory requirements.
- Dual authorization is required for payments exceeding a specified threshold set by the Board and the constitution.
- Regular financial reconciliations and audits shall be conducted to prevent fraud and mismanagement.

### 3.3 Revenue and Funding Sources

- GRIT Hub shall diversify its income sources, including grants, sponsorships, service revenue, and donations.
- Funding agreements must be reviewed and approved by the Finance Committee to ensure alignment with organizational goals.

### 3.4 Procurement and Expenditure

- Procurement processes must adhere to principles of fairness, transparency, and cost-effectiveness.
- Competitive bidding shall be required for purchases above a threshold set by the Board.
- Conflict of interest policies shall be enforced in procurement decisions.

## 4. Compliance and Reporting

### 4.1 Financial Reporting

- GRIT Hub shall prepare and publish annual financial statements audited by an independent auditor.
- Annual financial reports shall be submitted to the Board for review.

### 4.2 Regulatory Compliance

- GRIT Hub shall comply with South African tax laws, financial regulations, and nonprofit governance standards.
- All statutory filings, including tax returns and financial disclosures, shall be submitted on time.

### 4.3 Risk Management

- A risk management framework shall be maintained to identify and mitigate financial and operational risks.
- Insurance policies shall be reviewed periodically to ensure adequate coverage.

## 5. Ethics and Accountability

### 5.1 Conflict of Interest

- Board members and employees must disclose any conflicts of interest and recuse themselves from related decisions.
- A conflict-of-interest register shall be maintained and updated regularly.

### 5.2 Code of Conduct

- All personnel must adhere to ethical business practices, avoiding fraud, corruption, and financial mismanagement.
- Whistleblower protection policies shall be in place to encourage reporting of unethical conduct.

### 5.3 Transparency and Stakeholder Engagement

- Key financial and governance information shall be shared with stakeholders, including donors, partners, and the public, through annual reports and stakeholder meetings.
- Open forums and feedback mechanisms shall be provided to ensure community involvement in decision-making.

## 6. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Board and updated as necessary to reflect changes in governance practices, financial regulations, and organizational needs.
- Amendments must be approved by a majority vote of the Board.
