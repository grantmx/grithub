## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for awarding certifications to learners who successfully complete training programs at the Garden Route Innovation & Technology Hub (GRIT Hub). It ensures the credibility, quality, and recognition of GRIT Hub’s certification process.

## 2. Certification Principles

- **Recognition of Achievement:** Certifications shall serve as formal recognition of knowledge, skills, and competencies acquired.
- **Quality Assurance:** Certifications shall be awarded based on rigorous assessment and evaluation.
- **Industry Relevance:** Certifications shall align with industry standards and employer expectations.
- **Transparency and Integrity:** The certification process shall be fair, transparent, and free from bias.

## 3. Certification Criteria

### 3.1 Eligibility Requirements

- Learners must successfully complete all required coursework, assessments, and projects in the respective training program.
- A minimum attendance or participation requirement may be set for specific courses.
- Learners must comply with the academic integrity policy, including avoiding plagiarism and misconduct.

### 3.2 Assessment and Evaluation

- Certifications shall be issued based on performance in summative assessments, practical evaluations, and/or capstone projects.
- Competency-based certifications may require learners to demonstrate skills through real-world applications.
- Learners must achieve the minimum passing grade or competency level specified in the course syllabus.

## 4. Types of Certifications

### 4.1 Certificate of Completion

- Awarded to learners who complete a training program but do not undergo formal assessments.
- Indicates participation and engagement in the course content.

### 4.2 Certificate of Competency

- Awarded to learners who successfully pass assessments and demonstrate mastery of course objectives.
- May be used for career advancement, skill verification, or further education.

### 4.3 Industry-Aligned Certifications

- Certifications issued in partnership with accredited institutions, industry bodies, or professional organizations.
- Designed to enhance employability and industry recognition.

## 5. Issuance and Verification

### 5.1 Certification Issuance

- Certifications shall be issued digitally or in print format upon successful completion of program requirements.
- Certificates shall include the learner’s name, course title, completion date, and an official signature or seal.
- Unique serial numbers or QR codes may be assigned for authenticity verification.

### 5.2 Verification and Authentication

- GRIT Hub shall maintain a secure database of issued certifications.
- Employers, institutions, or third parties may verify certifications through an official request process which is in writing via email to: **[workforce@grithub.org.za](mailto:workforce@grithub.org.za)**

## 6. Certification Validity and Renewal

### 6.1 Expiry and Renewal

- Certain certifications may have expiration dates, requiring periodic renewal or re-assessment.
- Renewal criteria shall be specified for certifications linked to industry standards or evolving technologies.

### 6.2 Revocation of Certification

- Certifications may be revoked if issued based on fraudulent activities, academic misconduct, or administrative errors.
- Affected individuals shall be notified of revocation and given an opportunity to appeal.

## 7. Appeals and Disputes

- Learners who believe their certification was unfairly withheld may submit an appeal.
- Appeals must be lodged in writing within a specified period after results are issued.
- The Appeals Committee shall review the case and provide a final decision.

## 8. Continuous Improvement

- The certification process shall be periodically reviewed to ensure relevance, credibility, and alignment with industry needs.
- Stakeholder feedback, including input from learners, employers, and trainers, shall be incorporated into policy updates.

## 9. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Executive Team and updated as needed.
- Amendments must be approved by the Board of Directors before implementation.
