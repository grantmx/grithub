## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy provides a structured framework for learners, trainees, and stakeholders to formally appeal decisions related to assessments, certifications, disciplinary actions, and other administrative matters at the Garden Route Innovation & Technology Hub (GRIT Hub). It ensures fairness, transparency, and due process in handling appeals.

## 2. Grounds for Appeal

A learner or stakeholder may submit an appeal on the following grounds:

- **Assessment Appeals:** Disputes over grades, evaluation processes, or competency-based results.
- **Certification Appeals:** If a certification was denied due to alleged failure to meet requirements.
- **Disciplinary Appeals:** Contesting disciplinary actions, including academic misconduct or ethical violations.
- **Administrative Appeals:** Concerns over procedural errors or unfair administrative decisions.

## 3. Appeals Process

### 3.1 Lodging an Appeal

- Appeals must be submitted in writing within **seven (7) working days** from the date the decision was communicated.
- The appeal must include:
  - Full name and contact details of the appellant.
  - Detailed explanation of the issue and grounds for appeal.
  - Supporting evidence (if applicable).
  - Desired resolution or outcome.
- Appeals should be addressed to the **Appeals Committee** via the designated GRIT Hub contact channels.

### 3.2 Acknowledgment and Initial Review

- The Appeals Committee shall acknowledge receipt of the appeal within **three (3) working days**.
- An initial review shall be conducted to determine if the appeal meets the criteria for consideration.
- If the appeal lacks sufficient information, the appellant shall be given an opportunity to provide additional details.

### 3.3 Appeals Committee Review

- A panel of **at least three (3) independent members** shall review the appeal, ensuring impartiality.
- The committee may:
  - Interview the appellant and relevant parties.
  - Review assessment records, policies, and evidence submitted.
  - Seek expert opinions (if applicable).
- The review process shall be completed within **ten (10) working days** of receiving the full appeal submission.

### 3.4 Decision and Notification

- The Appeals Committee shall issue a formal written decision, outlining:
  - The outcome of the appeal (upheld, denied, or modified decision).
  - Justification for the decision.
  - Any further actions to be taken, if applicable.
- The decision shall be communicated to the appellant within **two (2) working days** after the review is concluded.

## 4. Final Appeal

- If the appellant is dissatisfied with the decision, they may submit a **final appeal** to the Executive Director within **five (5) working days** of receiving the committee’s decision.
- The Executive Director shall conduct an independent review and provide a final ruling within **seven (7) working days**.
- The Executive Director’s decision shall be final and binding.

## 5. Confidentiality and Protection

- All appeal proceedings shall be treated confidentially.
- No appellant shall face discrimination or retaliation for submitting an appeal.

## 6. Review and Amendments

- This policy shall be reviewed annually to ensure alignment with best practices and stakeholder needs.
- Amendments must be approved by the Board of Directors before implementation.
